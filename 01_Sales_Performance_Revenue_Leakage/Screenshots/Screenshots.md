## Dashboard & Business Insights

The Power BI dashboard translates the analytical findings into an interactive view of sales performance, enabling users to identify conversion gaps and prioritize commercial opportunities.

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of the sales funnel, realized revenue, open pipeline, and overall conversion performance.

![Executive Overview](Screenshots.md/01_Overview.png)

### 2. Sales Funnel Analysis

Breaks down opportunities across Won, Lost, and Engaging stages to identify where revenue is being lost throughout the commercial funnel.

![Sales Funnel](Screenshots.md/02_Sales_Funnel.png)

### 3. Product Performance

Compares products based on conversion performance and commercial contribution, highlighting both volume- and value-driven performance gaps.

![Product Analysis](Screenshots.md/03_Product_Analysis.png)

### 4. Sector Performance

Identifies sectors with the highest revenue leakage and the greatest opportunities for conversion improvement.

![Sector Analysis](Screenshots.md/04_Sector_Analysis.png)

### 5. Sales Agent Performance

Evaluates sales agents across win rate, opportunity volume, lost revenue, and product mix to identify performance gaps and potential areas for targeted intervention.

![Sales Agent Analysis](Screenshots.md/05_Sales_Agent_Analysis.png)

### 6. Management Focus

Combines product, sector, and sales-agent performance to identify the areas where management should prioritize sales resources to accelerate revenue growth.

![Management Focus](Screenshots.md/06_Management_Focus.png)
