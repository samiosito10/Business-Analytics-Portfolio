# Sales Performance & Revenue Leakage Analysis

### Business Analytics Project | SQL · PostgreSQL · Power BI · DAX

## Business Question

**Where should management focus its sales resources to accelerate revenue growth?**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a sales pipeline to identify **revenue leakage, conversion gaps, and commercial performance drivers** across products, sectors, and sales agents.

Using **SQL and PostgreSQL** for data exploration and analysis, and **Power BI and DAX** for interactive visualization, the project translates raw sales data into actionable business insights and management recommendations.

## Key Insights

* **€5M in revenue leakage** identified across the sales pipeline
* **MG Advanced** shows a volume-driven performance problem
* **GTX Pro and GTX Plus Pro** represent a high-value conversion problem
* **Retail, Technology, and Medical** are the sectors with the highest revenue leakage
* **MG Advanced × Medical, Retail, and Technology** emerge as priority Product × Sector combinations
* Sales-agent performance was assessed across **win rate, lost revenue, opportunity volume, and product mix**

## Management Takeaway

The analysis suggests that the greatest growth opportunity lies not simply in generating more pipeline, but in **improving conversion where revenue leakage is highest**.

Management should prioritize targeted commercial interventions across the most critical **Product × Sector combinations** and address performance gaps among sales agents.
