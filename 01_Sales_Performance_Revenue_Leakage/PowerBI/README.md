# Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

Interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales performance and identify revenue leakage across the commercial pipeline.

The dashboard transforms the SQL analysis into an interactive management tool, allowing users to explore performance across **products, sectors, and sales agents**.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of:

* Sales funnel performance
* Realized revenue
* Open pipeline
* Lost revenue potential
* Overall conversion performance

### 2. Sales Funnel

Analyzes the distribution of opportunities across **Won, Lost, and Engaging** stages to identify where revenue is being lost.

### 3. Product Performance

Evaluates products based on:

* Win Rate
* Opportunity Volume
* Revenue
* Lost Revenue Potential

The analysis highlights the difference between **volume-driven** and **value-driven** performance problems.

### 4. Sector Performance

Identifies sectors with the highest levels of revenue leakage and the greatest potential for conversion improvement.

### 5. Sales Agent Performance

Compares sales agents across:

* Win Rate
* Opportunity Volume
* Lost Revenue
* Product Mix

This helps identify sales resources that may require targeted intervention.

### 6. Management Focus

Combines **Product × Sector × Sales Agent** analysis to identify the areas where management should prioritize sales resources.

The analysis highlights the highest-priority commercial combinations based on revenue leakage and conversion performance.

## Key Management Insight

The dashboard shows that the largest growth opportunity does not necessarily come from generating more pipeline.

Instead, management should focus on **converting existing opportunities where revenue leakage is highest**.

> **Convert more. Don't just sell more.**
